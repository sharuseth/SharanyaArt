The artist
Sharanya

Sharanya has always loved art. Today she works mostly in oil, but also in poetry, prose and code.

She has just finished at the London School of Economics, where her academic interests spanned critical consciousness, critical pedagogies, decision ethics and gender economics. She previously ran an NGO centred on Amartya Sen's aspiration gap in South Asia, and is a data scientist by training. Her mother wouldn't let her have a pet, so she channels it into being a very proud elder sister, and loves logographic languages, Japanese lately.

As a painter she is self-taught, later refining her technique through atelier study in Prague. Her work is recognisable for a bespoke mastery of colour, hue and value. A perfectionist, she often works with a size 0 brush, layering patiently over months to build a depth and charisma that are hard to place. A single small painting can hold over three hundred individual colours.

Her debut poetry collection, on memory and stillness, follows this September.

"If only the birds who were good at singing sang, the forest would be very quiet."
