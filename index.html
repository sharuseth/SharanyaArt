The artist
Sharanya

Life got very busy and I felt like I was forgetting who I was. I picked up a brush again. Turns out that helped.

I live on a little hill in London. I paint florals, seascapes, and whatever whispers to me on a given afternoon. Colour is my love language and I've always secretly wanted to live in the Victorian era — you'll notice.

Most of my life I've painted for the people I love. I'm learning, slowly and joyfully, to paint for myself too. These are those paintings.

I'm also a student economist by day, which means every single purchase genuinely means the world.
