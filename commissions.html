Commissions
Something made for you

Finding my way as a young banker in a big city keeps me busy, but I do take the occasional commission. It isn't too strict, just room to make something true.

I want my art to be accessible, and I understand budgets vary. I work across a range of budgets, and each piece gets the same care, attention and focus. A little laxity with timelines is what lets me deliver something that resonates, something alive and true.

I'm happy to explore direction, but if you're lost and want a sounding board, drop me a line. I can help bring a vision to life by mapping the painting to your space.

i

Conversation

Tell me about the space, the vision, anything already calling to you: Pinterest boards, old photographs you'd want to immortalise. Then a quick call to elaborate.

ii

I begin painting

I keep you posted along the way, sending milestones as the piece comes to life.

iii

Three to six months

All paintings take their time to dry and settle. I usually aim to be done early.

iv

It's coming home

I send you final photographs to make sure you're happy with the piece. Framing if you'd like it, and careful shipping to your door. Oil paintings can stay in your family for decades, so I'll guide you on how to best care for your piece.

I usually reply within 40 to 72 hours. Prefer to talk? Message me on WhatsApp.
